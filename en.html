About

Studying Medical Informatics made me curious about one question: how do we handle clinical data and biosignals safely, and in a way that actually means something?

To explore it, I build things across layers — sensor control with Arduino, cloud design on Azure, and data analysis with Python and AI.

I completed Rakuten’s hackathon-style internship “Natsu no Jin”, and keep sharpening algorithms and data analysis on AtCoder and Kaggle.
